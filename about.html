Who We Are

SOLIDMOVE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED is a technology, software and systems architecture company registered in Nigeria. We operate as an enterprise technology backbone for organisations that need systems built, integrated and run properly.

Our work runs across two divisions. The first designs, deploys and supports enterprise hardware and software infrastructure: fleet telematics, edge security hardware, workflow middleware and managed cloud. The second is the Steph-Tech Ecosystem, our governed digital platform, controlled through SolidMove OS.

One company. Two divisions. One foundation.

Our Two Divisions

Enterprise infrastructure on one side, a governed digital platform on the other.

DIVISION 1 — ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE & HARDWARE

Solidmove FleetIQ™ — IoT telematics and real-time asset and fleet tracking with AI-driven diagnostics.

Solidmove CloudFlow™ — middleware for enterprise workflow automation, API integration and system pipelines.

Solidmove SecureEdge™ — edge computing hardware for zero-trust access control and biometric authentication.

Solidmove CoreCare™ — 24/7 managed cloud infrastructure and IT compliance services.

Core activity: enterprise hardware infrastructure integration.

DIVISION 2 — THE STEPH-TECH ECOSYSTEM

Pyramide & DPY Core — secure backend infrastructure: identity, access control and cryptographic gateways.

OnSpace — unified workspace and control-room interface.

OSA / SolidMove AI — conversational AI companions and natural-language layers.

PayClarity & SecureFlow — financial technology and transaction processing.

DiNG Portal — live streaming, interactive broadcast and community utilities.

Governed through SolidMove OS.
