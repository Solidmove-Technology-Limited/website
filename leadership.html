Meet Our Founder

The vision behind the Steph-Tech ecosystem.

SO

Stephen Osabuohien

FOUNDER & CEO

Solidmove Technology Limited
Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria

Stephen Osabuohien is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of SOLIDMOVE TECHNOLOGY LIMITED, a technology holding company based in Edo State, Nigeria.

With a deep passion for artificial intelligence, systems architecture, and financial technology, Stephen leads the development of the Steph-Tech Ecosystem — a fully integrated stack comprising AI (SolidMove AI), fintech (PayClarity), security (UMBRAE), and infrastructure.

Stephen believes that Africa's future lies in homegrown technology solutions that are fast, secure, and built with local challenges in mind. Under his leadership, SOLIDMOVE is on a mission to become the continent's most trusted technology partner.

"Technology must serve people, not the other way around. At SOLIDMOVE, we build with purpose, integrity, and a vision for Africa."

— Stephen Osabuohien, Founder & CEO
